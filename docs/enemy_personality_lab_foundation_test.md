# Enemy Personality Laboratory Foundation Test

This test verifies the neutral, safe comparison baseline. Personality tuning is explicitly deferred.

1. Launch **Enemy Personality Laboratory** from the Development Control Center.
2. Do not move or attack.
3. Confirm all four identical Goblins begin moving immediately toward the harmless target in their own lane.
4. Confirm every lane has the same dimensions, obstacle, Goblin, start distance, and target distance.
5. Remain in the observation area for at least 15 seconds. Confirm no Goblin attacks and Grace loses no health.
6. Press **RESET** and confirm all four Goblins, lane targets, Grace, and health return to the opening state.

Expected result: the four lanes provide a repeatable mechanical baseline. No cautious, bold, skittish, or brute profile differences are evaluated in this version.
