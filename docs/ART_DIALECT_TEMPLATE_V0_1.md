# Blockout Contract

Godot blockouts should use basic placeholders to answer:

- traversal;
- scale;
- sightlines;
- camera;
- encounter geometry;
- landmark placement.

Do not attempt final rocks, plants, architecture, or prop modeling in the blockout unless the primitive itself is intentionally the final asset.

Unrelated systems such as HUD or combat presentation are not changed during environment-art studies unless explicitly included in the brief.
