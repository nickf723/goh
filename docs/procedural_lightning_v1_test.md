# Procedural Lightning v1

Run `res://scenes/levels/prototypes/prototype_element_vfx_gallery_v1.tscn`.

1. Visit the illuminated Lightning wing on the right side of the gallery.
2. Trigger Direct and confirm a focused fractal bolt connects the source orb to the collector.
3. Trigger Storm and confirm a thicker trunk produces many bounded branches.
4. Trigger Chain and confirm separate arcs connect the target sequence.
5. Trigger Circuit and confirm a short precise arc bridges the two terminals.
6. Trigger Water and confirm a vertical strike spreads into flattened surface forks while the water shader electrifies.
7. Cycle global intensity and compare thickness, jitter, branch density, and flash strength.
8. Toggle gallery slow motion and inspect ribbon flicker, branches, and impact light.
9. Cast Lightning Spark in a scene that equips it and confirm the old sphere is hidden behind a live procedural arc trail.
10. Clear the Lightning exhibit and press F8. All active arcs, counters, replay state, and global time should reset.

V1 intentionally defers thunder audio, weather clouds, scorch decals, destructive terrain, AC arc simulation, and full gameplay chain-target selection.
