# Progression Rewards v1

The five starter Codex challenges now expose runtime-consumer status and test routes.

- Trial by Flame activates Charged Firebolt in AbilityCaster.
- Live Wire activates Chain Lightning through SpellModifierRegistry.
- Shatterproof activates Piercing Ice Lance through SpellModifierRegistry.
- Kitchen Chemistry activates Alchemy Recipe Insight in the cauldron.
- Pack Scholar synchronizes Gremlin Pounce into SpeciesKnowledge and the familiar loadout.

Alchemy Recipe Insight classifies a selected two-ingredient mix as locked, waiting, unstable, promising, or stable before ingredients are consumed. Undiscovered valid formulas remain unnamed while still revealing the elemental treatment they require.
