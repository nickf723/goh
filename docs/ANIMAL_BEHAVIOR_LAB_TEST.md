# Animal Behavior Lab Test Guide

Run:

`res://scenes/levels/prototypes/animal_behavior_lab_v1.tscn`

## Suggested test pass

1. Watch Mallow the sheep near the pasture with threat mode off.
2. Select Mallow with `1`, press `H`, and confirm the overhead intention becomes Forage and the move becomes Graze.
3. Press `P`, approach Mallow as Grace, and confirm she flees away from Grace.
4. Select Bramble with `2`, clear drives with `C`, then press `H` and `K` separately to compare grazing and habitat-seeking pressure.
5. Select Ash or Cinder with `3` or `4`, press `J`, and watch for Pack Howl while both wolves are present.
6. Leave threat mode on and approach the wolves to observe hunting, pouncing, biting, or pack support choices.
7. Press the configured restart input and confirm positions, drives, selection, and threat mode reset.

## Readouts

Each animal's overhead label shows:

- Current intention
- Current move
- Hunger
- Fear
- Social need

The left panel shows the complete drive set for the currently selected animal.
