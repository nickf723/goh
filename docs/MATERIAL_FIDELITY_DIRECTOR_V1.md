# Material Fidelity Director v1

## Goal

Material Fidelity Director raises the close-range quality ceiling of authored Godot geometry without requiring external modeling or texturing software.

It upgrades selected `StandardMaterial3D` families with:

- world-space triplanar projection;
- larger continuous macro texture scale;
- procedural normal-map detail;
- procedural roughness breakup;
- category-specific wet/dry response;
- shared enhanced material variants.

```text
semantic StandardMaterial3D family + MaterialFidelityProfile
                            ↓
                 MaterialFidelityDirector3D
                            ↓
   shared enhanced rock / paving / masonry / wood / roof / soil materials
```

## Why world triplanar

The prototype environment uses many procedural boxes, cylinders and stretched spheres. Normal UV mapping can reveal primitive boundaries through texture stretching or discontinuous scale.

World triplanar mapping projects the same texture field across X, Y and Z and anchors it in world space. Adjacent rocks sharing a material therefore read more like pieces of one geological surface rather than individually textured primitives.

This is intentionally used only on opaque authored surface families. Transparent water, waterfall sheets, foam and foliage remain outside this pass.

## Procedural detail

Each semantic category receives a shared normal-detail `NoiseTexture2D` and a shared roughness-detail `NoiseTexture2D`.

Current categories include:

```text
rock
rock_wet
paving
paving_wet
masonry
trim
wood
bark
root
roof
soil
soil_wet
```

The textures are generated by Godot at runtime and cached by category. Normal maps use `NoiseTexture2D.as_normal_map`; roughness textures use a grayscale noise ramp.

Wet categories reduce scalar roughness and normal strength so moisture reads through smoother highlights rather than simply becoming darker.

## Resource-sharing rule

Material Fidelity must never create one material per mesh.

The cache key is:

```text
semantic category + original material resource instance
```

If 120 rocks share one original `hero_rock` material, they also share one enhanced rock material.

The profile defines a hard maximum shared-variant budget as a safety rail.

## Green Grotto integration

Profile:

```text
data/material_fidelity/green_grotto_material_fidelity.tres
```

Integration layer:

```text
scripts/levels/prototype_green_grotto_material_pass.gd
```

Green enrolls visible meshes by comparing their material resource to known semantic material families from:

```text
GreenGrottoMaterialLibrary
GreenGrottoDetailMaterialLibrary
GreenGrottoHeroMaterialLibrary
```

This means future replacement geometry can inherit the pass by reusing the same material family instead of depending on node names.

## Benchmark controls

The Green Grotto art target now exposes:

```text
F3  Material Fidelity ON/OFF
F4  Surface Story ON/OFF
F5  Environmental Motion ON/OFF
F6  Camera Director ON/OFF
F7  Lighting quality tier
```

F3 restores the exact original material resources captured at registration. Re-enabling restores the exact cached enhanced resources.

## Ownership boundary

Material Fidelity may:

- replace presentation materials on enrolled `MeshInstance3D` nodes;
- create shared procedural texture resources;
- alter PBR normal and roughness response;
- alter UV/triplanar projection settings.

Material Fidelity must not:

- alter mesh geometry;
- alter collision;
- change gameplay surface semantics;
- make a wet-looking decal or material physically slippery;
- own water rendering;
- modify transparent VFX;
- decide weather state.

## Validation

```text
res://scenes/tests/material_fidelity_director_smoke_test.tscn
```

The regression verifies:

- Green installs the material-fidelity pass;
- a broad surface set is enrolled;
- enhanced variants stay inside the shared-resource budget;
- rock materials use world triplanar projection;
- procedural normal and roughness maps are installed;
- meshes sharing one original/category also share one enhanced material;
- F3/OFF restores exact original material objects;
- water remains excluded;
- the Surface Story decal layer survives alongside the material upgrade.
