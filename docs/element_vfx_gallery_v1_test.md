# Element VFX Gallery v1

Run `scenes/levels/prototypes/prototype_element_vfx_gallery_v1.tscn`.

Confirm the Water bay is illuminated, the other fifteen bays are reserved, all three water stages are visible, and the rear waterfall and automatic previews keep the water moving.

Use the consoles to trigger and cycle the two exhibits, change intensity, pause automatic replay, reverse current, cycle water state, pause the waterfall, enable slow motion, and clear active effects.

Press F8. Normal time, Standard intensity, Calm water, forward flow, automatic replay, waterfall visibility, counters, and player position should reset.

V1 reserves the complete room architecture but only fills the Water wing. Advanced reflection, shoreline, underwater-camera, ocean, and other elemental presentation remain future work.
