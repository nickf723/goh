# Steam Burst v1 Focused Playtest

1. Launch the Elemental Reaction Laboratory.
2. Go to the rear-right Steam station.
3. Aim Ice Lance at the glowing side `SURFACE` catalyst.
4. Confirm the water and state panel become `FROZEN`.
5. Aim Firebolt at the same catalyst.
6. Confirm a large pale Steam Burst appears and the surface becomes `STEAMING`.
7. Watch the three nearby targets:
   - each target inside the pale radius guide should gain `steamed`;
   - each should lose one stance point;
   - each should be nudged outward;
   - the panel should list the targets caught.
8. Confirm the burst happens once, while the surface's steam state lingers briefly.
9. Place or observe a target beyond the radius guide and confirm it is not affected.
10. Reset with the violet console or F8 and confirm every target returns to its staged position with cleared status, stance, and force.

Creative questions:

- Does the reaction feel like Fire and Ice compounded into a third event?
- Is the radius easy to understand without staring at debug text?
- Is the shove satisfying without becoming slapstick?
- Does the cloud remain readable around multiple targets?
- Does the live panel clarify the experiment without dominating the room?
